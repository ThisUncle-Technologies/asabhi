Our Story

From humble beginnings to one of Tanzania's trusted supply companies — built on integrity and a commitment to quality.

Established 2012

The ASABHI Story

ASABHI Company Limited was founded in 2012 by Ibrahim Matayi with a simple but powerful idea: Tanzanian businesses deserved a supply partner they could actually rely on. Too often, companies were losing time and money chasing unreliable suppliers and inconsistent quality.

Starting with a small warehouse in Dar es Salaam, we built our catalogue one client relationship at a time. Today, ASABHI serves over 200 clients across Tanzania — from SMEs to multinationals and government institutions.

Our growth has been organic, driven entirely by referrals and long-term partnerships. Every client that stays with us is a testament to the promise we made in 2012: quality supplies, every time, on time.

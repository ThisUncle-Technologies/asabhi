Who We Are

Built on Trust. Driven by Quality.

Founded in 2012, ASABHI Company Limited has grown from a local trader to one of Tanzania's dependable general supply companies. We serve a diverse client base — from SMEs to large corporates and government institutions.

Our strength lies in our relationships: with trusted suppliers, with our clients, and with the people of Tanzania. Every order we fulfil is a commitment to quality and partnership.
